Where this guide comes from

Lecture 1 follows your supplied keywords. Lecture 2 follows the eight photos in img/lec2, with clearly marked extensions for the matching task-list questions. Exercise summaries and solutions refer to List 1; the solutions were prepared for this guide, rather than copied from an official answer key. Explanations were checked against the supplied course books and written in study-note form.

Montgomery & Runger · Applied Statistics and Probability for Engineers, 7th edition

Read §1.1.1 for variability (printed p. 3); §2.1 for random experiments, sample spaces, and event operations (pp. 18–23); §2.2 for counting (pp. 23–26); and §§2.3–2.4 for relative frequency, probability rules, and unions (from p. 26).

Open Chapter 2 at PDF page 36. De Morgan’s laws are on printed p. 23 / PDF p. 41; counting formulas on printed pp. 24–25 / PDF pp. 42–43.

Walpole, Myers, Myers & Ye · Probability & Statistics for Engineers & Scientists, 9th edition

The file named “503 STAT - Text Book” is this textbook. Read §§2.1–2.2 for sample spaces and events (printed pp. 35–42); §2.3 for counting, permutations, and combinations (pp. 44–51); and §§2.4–2.5 for probabilities and additive rules (pp. 52–59).

Open Chapter 2 at PDF page 56. Permutations begin on printed p. 47 / PDF p. 68; combinations on printed p. 50 / PDF p. 71.

The books use S for the sample space and A′ for a complement; this guide uses Ω and Aᶜ. They mean the same things. PDF page numbers count from the beginning of the file and differ from the printed page numbers. Newton’s symbol identities and the binomial expansion are additional explanations of your keyword. For Lecture 2, see Montgomery & Runger §2.5 (conditional probability, printed pp. 31–33; definition on PDF p. 51) and Walpole et al. §2.6 (from printed p. 62 / PDF p. 83).

The course description is reserved for future planning. This guide does not claim that all topics in the books or the later exercises have already been covered in your lectures.
